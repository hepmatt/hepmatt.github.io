Players

Win % is wins divided by games that person actually played, so missing game night does not hurt you. vs. luck compares wins to what pure chance would give in those same games (1 in 4 for a 4-player game, 1 in 2 for 2v2). 1.00x is average, 2.00x is twice as many wins as luck. Players with fewer than 3 games are listed below the ranked players.

Head to head

Only counts games where both people played, which is the fairest comparison when the group changes night to night. "Ahead" means finished in a better place.
